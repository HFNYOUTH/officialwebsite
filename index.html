So… what is HGYN?

Heartfulness Global Youth Network (HGYN) is a global community for young people, inspired by the Heartfulness approach to inner wellbeing.

We bring young people from different backgrounds, cultures and corners of the world together to connect, exchange ideas, explore wellbeing and grow together.

Think of it as a space to…

CONNECT

Meet people beyond your usual circles.

EXPLORE

Discover new perspectives, ideas and experiences.

CREATE

Turn ideas into conversations, projects and possibilities.

GROW

Build a deeper relationship with yourself and others.

Okay, but what is Heartfulness?

Heartfulness is a simple approach to inner wellbeing that encourages us to turn inward, connect with ourselves and create moments of stillness in everyday life.

HGYN brings this Heartfulness-inspired approach into a space created with and for young people.

    PAUSE

  2. 02

    Bring your attention back to yourself.

    Create a little more calm and clarity within.
